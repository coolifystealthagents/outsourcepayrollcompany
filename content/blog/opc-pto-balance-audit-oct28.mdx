## Why this control matters

PTO Balance Audit is a record, not a reminder to be careful. It shows what the team checked, who owned the next step, and what remained open before the client acted. The control matters because the employee is paid out for hours that were already used. For the payroll record, the analyst records the pay period, the employee or account, the source, and the named approver, and checks the same evidence before the pay date.

## Open the governing record

Start with the leave ledger and policy. Record the source, the version or date observed, the owner, and the field the evidence supports. Save the original rather than retyping it into a summary.
