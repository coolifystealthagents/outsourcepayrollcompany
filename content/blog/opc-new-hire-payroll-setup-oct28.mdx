## Why this control matters

New Hire Payroll Setup is a record, not a reminder to be careful. It shows what the team checked, who owned the next step, and what remained open before the client acted. The control matters because the employee starts but the first paycheck is missing a deduction or a state account. For the payroll record, the analyst records the pay period, the employee or account, the source, and the named approver, and checks the same evidence before the pay date.

## Open the governing record

Start with the payroll setup checklist. Record the source, the version or date observed, the owner, and the field the evidence supports. Save the original rather than retyping it into a summary. ## Where the handoff breaks

The predictable failure is this: the employee starts but the first paycheck is missing a deduction or a state account. It becomes expensive when the next owner cannot tell whether the item is a request, an approval, or a correction. Use one status set: ready, exception, approved, and closed. A status without an owner and a date is only a label.
