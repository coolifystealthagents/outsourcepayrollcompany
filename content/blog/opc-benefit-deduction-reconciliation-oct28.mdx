## Why this control matters

Benefit Deduction Reconciliation is a record, not a reminder to be careful. It shows what the team checked, who owned the next step, and what remained open before the client acted. The control matters because the invoice changes and the payroll deduction remains at the old amount. The predictable failure is this: the invoice changes and the payroll deduction remains at the old amount.
