## Why this control matters

Payroll Tax Filing Evidence is a record, not a reminder to be careful. It shows what the team checked, who owned the next step, and what remained open before the client acted. The control matters because the deposit clears but the filing confirmation is missing and the quarter looks late. For the payroll record, the analyst records the pay period, the employee or account, the source, and the named approver, and checks the same evidence before the pay date.

## Open the governing record

Start with the tax filing log. Record the source, the version or date observed, the owner, and the field the evidence supports. Save the original rather than retyping it into a summary. The assistant prepares and checks the record; the account owner keeps the decision that changes a client commitment, a payment, a schedule, or access. ## Where the handoff breaks

The predictable failure is this: the deposit clears but the filing confirmation is missing and the quarter looks late. It becomes expensive when the next owner cannot tell whether the item is a request, an approval, or a correction. Use one status set: ready, exception, approved, and closed.
