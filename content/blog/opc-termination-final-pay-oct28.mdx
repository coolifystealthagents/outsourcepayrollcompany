## Why this control matters

Termination Final Pay is a record, not a reminder to be careful. It shows what the team checked, who owned the next step, and what remained open before the client acted. The control matters because the final check is late because the deduction and PTO items were not calculated together. ## Where the handoff breaks

The predictable failure is this: the final check is late because the deduction and PTO items were not calculated together. It becomes expensive when the next owner cannot tell whether the item is a request, an approval, or a correction. Use one status set: ready, exception, approved, and closed. A status without an owner and a date is only a label.
