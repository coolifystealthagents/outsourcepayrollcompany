## Why this control matters

Payroll Work Queue Ownership is a record, not a reminder to be careful. It shows what the team checked, who owned the next step, and what remained open before the client acted. The control matters because the item is marked complete when the email is sent, not when the payroll system confirms it. For the payroll record, the analyst records the pay period, the employee or account, the source, and the named approver, and checks the same evidence before the pay date.

## Open the governing record

Start with the payroll queue and owner log. Record the source, the version or date observed, the owner, and the field the evidence supports. Save the original rather than retyping it into a summary. ## Where the handoff breaks

The predictable failure is this: the item is marked complete when the email is sent, not when the payroll system confirms it. It becomes expensive when the next owner cannot tell whether the item is a request, an approval, or a correction. Use one status set: ready, exception, approved, and closed. A status without an owner and a date is only a label.
