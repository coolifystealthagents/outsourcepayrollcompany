## Why this control matters

W-2 Year-End Reconciliation is a record, not a reminder to be careful. It shows what the team checked, who owned the next step, and what remained open before the client acted. The control matters because the W-2 total differs from the final payroll register and no one can explain the gap. For the payroll record, the analyst records the pay period, the employee or account, the source, and the named approver, and checks the same evidence before the pay date.

## Where the handoff breaks

The predictable failure is this: the W-2 total differs from the final payroll register and no one can explain the gap. It becomes expensive when the next owner cannot tell whether the item is a request, an approval, or a correction. Use one status set: ready, exception, approved, and closed. A status without an owner and a date is only a label.
